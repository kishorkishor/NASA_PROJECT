Fire detections per year · Bangladesh

About this page

Data

Fire detections from NASA FIRMS: MODIS Collection 6.1 (Aqua) and VIIRS S-NPP 375 m active-fire products, vegetation fires only, Bangladesh, January 2003 to June 2026. Terrain from SRTM-based tiles, people from WorldPop 2020, place names from OpenStreetMap, burn-scar checks from Sentinel-2 L2A.

Method

  • VIIRS detections are reduced to unique ~1 km cell-days, about the size of a MODIS pixel.
  • For each region (hills, plains) and season (Feb–May, rest of year) we learn how many MODIS detections match one VIIRS cell-day, using 2012–2023, the years when both flew and Aqua's orbit was stable. Aqua's 2022 safe-mode days are removed from both sensors first.
  • The calibration years are resampled 2,000 times for the 95% interval. It covers the conversion factors only, not missed fires.
  • Every ~2 km square in the hill districts is scored by fire × steepness × people. "Inspect first" means it stays in the top 50 in at least 80% of 54 scoring settings.

Limits

  • MODIS is the reference, not the truth. Both sensors miss small fires and fires under cloud.
  • This is an inspection list, not a landslide-risk map. No landslide records were used.
  • Jhum is a traditional livelihood. The list is meant for safety checks with hill communities and has not yet been reviewed by people who work there.
